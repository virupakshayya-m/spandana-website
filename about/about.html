About Spandana

Spandana is the annual cultural festival of our institution, celebrating creativity, diversity, and youthful energy. It provides a vibrant platform for students to showcase talent beyond academics and fosters unity through music, dance, art, and performance. Spandana is an experience that creates memories which stay forever.

Vision & Theme

Spandana 2026 envisions a celebration of unity in diversity. The festival aims to encourage cultural expression, leadership, and collaboration while nurturing confidence and creativity among students.
